✦ C R Y S T A L L I N E ✦
a JUPITERVISION sub-project

Somewhere between the source and the screen, detail is lost. Crystalline goes looking for it.

It begins with a Laplacian — a small, exact thing that finds the finest structures hiding in the signal. Luminance through the green channel alone, because the eye doesn't need more. What it finds gets lifted above the noise before it's ever asked to survive a resample.

Then the topology. Edges aren't blur lines — they have direction, valence, geometry. 2x2 diagonals resolve which way an edge truly runs. A 4x4 valence check balances the local neighborhood. Boolean becomes float without a single branch. Edges are blended, not smeared.

And at the end, the hardware takes over — fixed-function silicon sampling at full rate, a negative LOD bias pulling back the softness the filter leaves behind. Sharpness that survives.

8x8 tiles, the native rhythm of Intel Gen9/Gen11. Sixteen taps, no more. A millisecond, start to finish.

Part of the JUPITERVISION flagship ecosystem — where every sub-project earns its place in the whole.

The pipeline is invisible. That's the point. What you see is what was always there.
